Gift packaging, across the order journey

A standalone demonstration of a paid cart add-on. All products, prices, and orders below are invented. No real Shopify store or checkout is connected.

Shared cart state

Design and QA notes

  • Cart and minicart share the same state and reflect changes immediately.
  • Gift packaging is optional, paid, capped at one, and unavailable for an otherwise empty cart.
  • Removing the final ordinary product removes packaging automatically.
  • A demo checkout creates a fictional order report, without making a payment or contacting a store.
  • Checkbox, information disclosure, and navigation are keyboard-accessible.
Fieldstone Provisions is a fictional brand. This example is independently authored and is not a copy of any merchant implementation.
